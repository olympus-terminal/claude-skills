# Suno Luminous Focus

Create instrumental ambience that feels melancholy yet illuminated: emotionally deep, technologically unfamiliar, precise, and useful for prolonged concentration. Put novelty in timbre while keeping harmony, rhythm, dynamics, and form stable enough for focus.

## Mandatory Record Behavior

- Save every proposed or revised prompt set as a new timestamped `.txt` file in the current workspace.
- Use a traceable filename such as `suno_luminous_focus_prompts_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.txt`. Never overwrite an earlier record.
- Never print prompt bodies in chat unless the user explicitly asks to see them there.
- After saving, return only the clickable file link and a concise summary of the number and broad musical directions. Do not quote excerpts or list full prompt titles.
- If saving fails, report the failure without exposing the prompt bodies.
- Include the creation timestamp, request summary, intended Suno workflow, descriptive titles, complete Style text, and applicable Exclude text inside the record.

## Suno Workflow

Prepare records for Custom mode with Instrumental enabled. Put each musical prompt in Style of Music and put unwanted elements in Advanced Options -> Exclude. Leave Lyrics unused. Keep exclusions out of the positive Style prompt where possible; use a shared exclusion block plus prompt-specific additions only when needed.

Do not rely on film, game, composer, or artist names. Translate references into musical properties.

## Prompt Anatomy

For each prompt, define:

- The listening function: sustained concentration, low interruption, and stable intensity.
- Luminous melancholy through restrained modal or extended-minor harmony, suspended color tones, controlled ambiguity, and slow harmonic movement.
- Tempo or beatless pacing, event density, repetition cycle, and degree of gradual evolution.
- One unobtrusive foundation such as a pedal tone, slow pulse, bass ostinato, or stable drone.
- One primary invented synth timbre and, only when useful, one secondary invented accent.
- Each novel timbre's attack, body, decay, register, spectrum, modulation, stereo behavior, and musical role.
- Mix behavior: clean frequency separation, restrained transients, bounded reverb, stable low end, and controlled stereo depth.
- A continuous development rule based on slow timbral change rather than sectional drama.

## Sonic Invention

- Describe unheard-of sounds through coherent audible behavior, not stacks of decorative adjectives.
- Combine a physical or material analogy with synthesis behavior such as wavetable flexing, modal resonance, phase rotation, granular decay, formant movement, spectral migration, or controlled wavefolding.
- Give every invented sound one clear musical job and keep its behavior predictable over a named cycle.
- Preserve a fixed or tightly controlled fundamental when upper harmonics move. Keep sub-bass mono and stable when high-frequency material widens or drifts.
- Let dark fundamentals support sparse luminous partials. Novelty should reward close listening without pulling attention away from work.
- Vary the synthesis system materially across a set rather than paraphrasing the same pad texture.

## Anti-Fluff and Focus Constraints

Avoid generic `dreamy`, `ethereal`, `lush`, or `cinematic` language unless it is replaced by audible behavior. Exclude featureless pad washes, reverberant haze, wellness or spa cues, fantasy sparkle, sentimental solo piano, saxophone, ornamental bells, wind chimes, vocal-like hooks, choir, spoken word, and lyrics.

Avoid catchy lead melodies, busy percussion, trap hats, lo-fi beats, trailer drums, risers, drops, large crescendos, sudden register changes, sharp transients, excessive stereo movement, sub-bass surges, dense arpeggiation, and long inventories of instruments or genre labels.

Prefer low event density, patient repetition, stable loudness, clean spectral layers, subtle internal movement, and enough structural motion to prevent inertness.

## Validation Before Saving

Confirm that:

1. Every entry is instrumental and focus-compatible.
2. Luminous melancholy is expressed through harmony, timbre, and spectral contrast rather than merely named.
3. Every invented timbre has a precise audible description and arrangement role.
4. No prompt contains more than two signature invented timbres.
5. Dynamics remain stable and development avoids climaxes or abrupt events.
6. The set contains materially different sonic systems rather than cosmetic variants.
7. Style and Exclude content are clearly separated.
8. The timestamped record exists, is non-empty, and contains the complete set.
9. The chat response contains no prompt bodies.

Prompt design does not prove the rendered result. Do not claim that a sound or track succeeded until the generated audio has been heard and evaluated.
